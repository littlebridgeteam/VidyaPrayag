Privacy Policy
Last updated: July 14, 2026
1. Information We Collect
ZeroW collects phone number, name, GPS location (when you submit a bin report), and photos of waste bins. Sweepers additionally provide Aadhaar/ID number for payment verification.
2. How We Use Your Data
Your data is used to: route cleaning jobs to the nearest sweeper, verify before/after cleaning proofs, process payments, calculate zone cleanliness scores, and display leaderboard rankings. We never sell your data to third parties.
3. Location Data
GPS coordinates are captured only when you actively submit a report or when a sweeper checks in at a job site. Location is not tracked in the background. You can disable location access in your phone settings at any time.
4. Photo Data
Photos submitted as bin reports or cleaning proofs are stored securely on our servers and used solely for verification. Photos may be reviewed by municipal admin staff. Photos are deleted 90 days after the associated report is resolved.
5. Payment Data (Sweepers)
Sweeper bank account details and Aadhaar numbers are encrypted at rest and used only for processing weekly payments via our payment partner. This data is never shared with third parties except as required by Indian tax law.
6. Data Retention
Account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by writing to privacy@zerow.app. Deletion is processed within 30 days.
7. Your Rights
Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@zerow.app.
8. Contact
Questions about this policy? Email privacy@zerow.app or write to: ZeroW, Sector 12, Aligarh, UP 202001.

Points are credited for verified reports only. Points have no monetary value and can be redeemed for rewards at ZeroW's discretion. ZeroW reserves the right to adjust point values or expire unused points after 12 months.

Sweeper Terms
Last updated: July 14, 2026
1. Registration & Verification
Sweepers must provide valid government-issued ID (Aadhaar) and a verified phone number. Registration is subject to approval by your zone supervisor. ZeroW does not employ sweepers directly — you are engaged as an independent service provider.
2. Job Assignment
Jobs are auto-assigned based on GPS proximity and current workload. You may accept or decline jobs, but declining more than 3 jobs per day may reduce your assignment priority.
3. Proof of Cleaning
You must submit before and after photos for each job. Photos must clearly show the bin and surrounding area. Jobs without valid proof will not be paid.
4. Payment Terms
Payments are calculated per verified job and transferred weekly to your registered bank account. Payment rates are set by the municipal authority and may vary by zone. Minimum payout threshold: ₹100. Disputes must be raised within 7 days of payment.
5. Conduct & Safety
Sweepers must: wear provided safety gear, follow traffic rules while commuting, maintain professional conduct with citizens, and not damage public or private property. Safety violations result in immediate suspension.
6. Equipment
Cleaning equipment (broom, dustbin bags, gloves) is provided by the municipal authority. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to your supervisor within 24 hours.
7. Termination
Sweeper accounts may be terminated for: false proof submissions, repeated safety violations, absenteeism (no check-in for 7 consecutive days), or fraudulent payment claims. Terminated sweepers may appeal to their zone supervisor within 15 days.
8. Contact
Questions? Contact your zone supervisor or email sweepers@zerow.app.
